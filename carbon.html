Standard Operating Procedure

End-to-End Blue Carbon MRV Lifecycle

Ensuring scientific rigor, equitable farmer-industry partnerships, and tamper-evident carbon credit issuance.

01

Farmer & Land Linkage Filing

Industry partners or coastal landholders submit partnership agreements, farmer identity details, land ownership deeds, and geo-tagged coastal plantation imagery.

Applicant Portal Submission
02

NCCR Scientific & Document Audit

Authorized NCCR verifiers inspect submitted land records, validate coastal coordinates and field photographs, and quantify net carbon sequestration (tCO₂e).

NCCR Verifier Console
03

Credit Issuance & Hash Attestation

Upon approval, NCCR mints verified carbon credits and issues a unique cryptographic verification hashcode directly to the applicant's registry account.
